Which format should you convert to?

JPG / JPEG

A widely compatible format for photographs. JPG does not support transparency; transparent areas are flattened onto a white background when creating JPG output.

PNG

Useful when you need lossless image output or transparency. PNG files can be larger than lossy formats for photographic images.

WebP

A modern web image format that can offer smaller files. Check that the apps or websites where you plan to use the image support WebP.

Convert common image formats

JPG to WebP

Convert JPG photos to WebP for web use when your destination supports it. You can also adjust compression to balance file size and visual quality.

PNG to JPG

Convert PNG images to JPG for compatible photo workflows and smaller files. Note that transparency is replaced with a white background in JPG output.

WebP to PNG

Convert WebP images to PNG when a workflow requires PNG output. PNG may result in a larger file, particularly for photographs.

JPG to PNG

Save a JPG image as PNG when your workflow specifically needs the PNG format. Converting formats does not restore image detail lost in the original JPG.

Frequently asked questions

Is the image converter free?

Yes, SmartCompress is free to use.

Which image formats can I convert?

JPG, PNG and WebP are supported. Select a supported output format in the tool.

Will converting reduce image quality?

It depends on the selected format and compression settings. Lossy JPG or WebP output can reduce image data; PNG output is generally lossless for the encoding step but may increase file size.

Are images uploaded to a server?

Selected images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to SmartCompress servers for conversion.

Can I convert several images at once?

Yes. You can process a batch of up to 20 images and download individual results or a ZIP.
