Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

SmartCompress is designed to process images locally in your browser. When you select images, the compression and conversion work is performed on your device; selected image files are not sent to a SmartCompress upload server.

Files you select

Selected files are used by the page for image processing, previews and downloads. They remain in your browser session and are cleared when you remove them, clear the page, or close the session. Avoid using the tool on files if your device or browser is shared or compromised.

Advertising and cookies

SmartCompress may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve, personalize (where permitted), limit repetition of, and measure ads. Google and its partners may use information about your visits to this site or other sites to provide relevant advertising, subject to your settings and applicable law.

You can manage Google personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and opt out of some third-party personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Where required by applicable privacy laws, consent choices will be presented using the appropriate consent mechanism before relevant advertising technologies are used.

Analytics and cookies

Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. SmartCompress does not use selected image files for advertising or analytics.

Third-party services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Your browser may communicate with the hosting provider to load the website. Advertising requests may also be sent to Google and other advertising partners. Review their privacy documentation for information about their data practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the website owner through the public project repository.
