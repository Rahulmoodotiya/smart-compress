Compress Images for WhatsApp

When you want to share a photo quickly, a smaller file can be easier to send, store and download. Compress a copy before sharing when file size matters.

Keep a copy of the original

Compression may reduce fine details. Keep your original image if you may need a full-quality version later.

Photos and screenshots

For camera photos, JPG or WebP can reduce size substantially. For screenshots with text or transparent graphics, PNG may preserve edges better. If converting transparent PNG to JPG, transparent areas become white.

Resize for the recipient

If the recipient only needs to view the image on a phone, consider reducing its dimensions. Keep the aspect ratio enabled so the image is not distorted.

Steps

Open SmartCompress, select one or more images, choose the format and quality, resize if needed, compress, and download the results. Then attach the saved files in WhatsApp.

WhatsApp image quality

Messaging apps may apply their own image processing when photos are sent normally. If preserving more detail matters, check the app's document/file sharing option and the available HD setting.
