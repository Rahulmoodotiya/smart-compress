Convert JPG, PNG and WebP

Different image formats suit different tasks. SmartCompress can export selected images as JPG, PNG, WebP or Auto/original format directly in your browser.

JPG to PNG

PNG output can preserve edges and is useful for graphics, but converting a JPG to PNG does not restore detail lost in the original JPG. The PNG file may be larger.

PNG to JPG

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent pixels are flattened onto a white background during JPG conversion. JPG is generally suitable for photographs.

WebP conversion

WebP supports lossy and lossless-style image encoding and can support transparency. It is useful for web images, but check compatibility with the destination app or service.

Auto / original

Use Auto to keep each selected file's original format. This is helpful when processing a mixed group of JPG, PNG and WebP files and you want to avoid unnecessary format changes.

How to convert

Choose files, select the desired output format, adjust quality for supported formats, and compress. Open the output to verify the format and appearance before using it.
