Frequently Asked Questions

Is SmartCompress free?

Yes. The image compressor is free to use.

Are my images uploaded?

No image upload server is used by the current compression workflow. Processing happens in your browser on your device.

Which formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP can be selected. You can choose JPG, PNG, WebP or Auto/original for output.

How many images can I process at once?

Up to 20 images in a batch. Very large batches or files may be limited by your device memory.

Why can a PNG be larger after compression?

PNG is lossless. Re-encoding does not always reduce size, and the quality slider does not control PNG file size. If suitable, convert a photo to JPG or WebP.

Does JPG keep transparent backgrounds?

No. JPG has no transparency channel; transparent areas are flattened against white when converted to JPG.

Can I resize?

Yes. Choose a preset or enter custom dimensions. Keep aspect ratio enabled to avoid distortion.

How do I compress an image without losing visible quality?

Start with a high quality setting and compare the preview with the original. If the image is oversized, resize it to the dimensions you actually need before lowering quality. Lossless formats such as PNG preserve pixel data, while JPG and WebP can trade some detail for smaller files.

What is the best format for photos?

JPG is widely compatible and practical for photographs. WebP can often produce smaller files at similar visual quality. Use PNG when transparency, sharp text or lossless graphics are important.

What image size is good for a website?

It depends on display dimensions and image content. Resize oversized photos to the largest dimensions they will actually be displayed at, then test a visually acceptable compression level. There is no single KB target that fits every image.

Why does my compressed image look blurry?

Quality may be set too low, or the image may have been resized too small. Try a higher quality value and check the result at its intended display size.

Can I compress images on my phone?

Yes, SmartCompress works in modern mobile browsers. Processing large images or batches can use significant memory, so try fewer images if your device slows down.

Does compression change image dimensions?

Compression quality usually affects file size and visual detail, not dimensions. Resizing is a separate option that changes width and height.

Can I download all results together?

Yes, when batch results are ready, you can create a ZIP archive of the processed images. You can also download images individually.

Why might the target size not be reached?

Some image content and quality settings cannot achieve an exact target. The tool reports the closest result where supported.
