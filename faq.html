Frequently Asked Questions

Is SmartCompress free?

Yes. The image compressor is free to use.

Are my images uploaded?

No image upload server is used by the current compression workflow. Processing happens in your browser on your device.

Which formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP can be selected. You can choose JPG, PNG, WebP or Auto/original for output.

How many images can I process at once?

Up to 20 images in a batch. Very large batches or files may be limited by your device memory.

Why can a PNG be larger after compression?

PNG is lossless. Re-encoding does not always reduce size, and the quality slider does not control PNG file size. If suitable, convert a photo to JPG or WebP.

Does JPG keep transparent backgrounds?

No. JPG has no transparency channel; transparent areas are flattened against white when converted to JPG.

Can I resize?

Yes. Choose a preset or enter custom dimensions. Keep aspect ratio enabled to avoid distortion.

Why might the target size not be reached?

Some image content and quality settings cannot achieve an exact target. The tool reports the closest result where supported.
