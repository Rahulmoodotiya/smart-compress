How to Compress JPG Images

JPG is a popular format for photographs because it can produce relatively small files. SmartCompress lets you reduce JPG size in your browser without uploading the selected photo.

Steps

  1. Open SmartCompress and select JPG/JPEG images.
  2. Choose JPG as the output format or Auto to retain the source format.
  3. Adjust quality. Lower quality generally produces smaller files, but can introduce visible artifacts.
  4. Optional: resize the image to smaller dimensions.
  5. Compress, review the preview and file-size result, then download.

Quality tips

Start around 75–85% quality for a balance, then compare the preview. Images with fine text, sharp edges or gradients may need higher quality. Resize large images to their actual display dimensions before compressing.

Batch processing

Select up to 20 images for one batch. Download each result or create a ZIP when the total output size is within the tool's safety limit.
