How to compress JPG images

  1. Select JPG or JPEG photos from your device.
  2. Adjust the quality setting to balance file size and visual detail.
  3. Optionally resize the image to smaller pixel dimensions if the original is larger than you need.
  4. Process your image and review the result.
  5. Download the compressed JPG, or batch process several images and download a ZIP.

Choose a compression balance

Keep more detail

Use a higher quality setting when preserving fine detail is important. The resulting file may be larger.

Smaller file

Try a lower quality setting when file size is your priority. Inspect the image for blur, blockiness or other compression artifacts.

Resize as well

If the image's pixel dimensions are larger than its intended display size, reducing dimensions can also lower the final file size.

Why compress JPGs?

Faster uploads

Smaller files may be easier to upload to websites, forms and messaging services with file-size limits.

Web performance

Optimizing image dimensions and file sizes can help avoid sending unnecessarily large photos to website visitors.

Save storage

Compression can help reduce the space used by copies of photos, depending on the quality setting and original file.

Frequently asked questions

How can I compress a JPG image?

Select a JPG image, adjust compression quality, optionally resize it, process and download the result.

Is JPG compression free?

Yes. SmartCompress is free to use in your browser.

Can I compress multiple JPGs at once?

Yes, batch processing supports up to 20 images at a time.

Will compression affect image quality?

JPG compression is lossy. Lower quality settings can reduce file size further but may create visible artifacts. Review the result before downloading.

Are my JPG files uploaded?

Images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to SmartCompress servers for processing.
