How to resize an image

  1. Open the SmartCompress tool and select your image or batch of images.
  2. Choose a resize preset or enter a custom width and height in pixels.
  3. Enable the aspect-ratio option if you want the dimensions linked proportionally.
  4. Choose output format and compression settings if needed.
  5. Process and download your resized images.

When should you resize an image?

Website images

Use dimensions appropriate for the space where an image will appear. Avoid serving unnecessarily large dimensions when a smaller display size is sufficient.

Social and sharing

Prepare image dimensions for a design or sharing workflow. Confirm the destination's current dimension recommendations before exporting.

Forms and uploads

Resize images to meet a required pixel dimension when an upload form or application specifies width and height limits.

Resize and compress privately

Selected files are processed locally in your browser. SmartCompress does not upload your image files to its servers for processing. You can also compress or convert supported images during the same workflow.
